# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime commitment

Northwind Cloud commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Team plans and 99.99% monthly uptime for Enterprise plans. Starter plans have no uptime commitment.

Scheduled maintenance windows are announced at least 72 hours in advance on the status page and do not count as downtime. Maintenance normally occurs on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers are eligible for service credits. Uptime between 99.0% and the commitment earns a 10% credit on that month's fee. Uptime below 99.0% earns a 25% credit. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month by contacting support.

Service credits are applied to future invoices and cannot be exchanged for cash. The maximum credit in any month is 25% of the monthly fee.
