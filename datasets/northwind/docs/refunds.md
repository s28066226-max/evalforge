# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation

Workspaces can be cancelled from the admin console under Settings > Plan > Cancel subscription. Only workspace owners can cancel; admins cannot.

After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days, during which the owner can reactivate the workspace with all content intact. After 30 days, all workspace data is permanently deleted.

## Refunds

Monthly plans are non-refundable, but cancelling stops all future charges. Annual plans can be refunded on a prorated basis if cancelled within the first 60 days of the term. After 60 days, annual plans are not refundable.

Refund requests are processed within 10 business days and are returned to the original payment method.

## Downgrades

Downgrading from Team to Starter takes effect at the end of the current billing period. If the workspace uses more than 50 GB of storage, the downgrade is blocked until usage is reduced below the Starter limit.
