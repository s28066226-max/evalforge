# Customer Support

## Channels

Starter customers can get help through the community forum and email support. Team customers additionally get live chat. Enterprise customers get a dedicated customer success manager and a private Slack channel.

## Response times

Email support responds within 2 business days for Starter, within 8 business hours for Team, and within 1 hour for Enterprise priority-1 incidents. Support business hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC.

Enterprise priority-1 incidents are handled 24/7, including weekends and holidays.

## Severity levels

Priority-1 means the service is unavailable or data loss is occurring. Priority-2 means a major feature is degraded. Priority-3 covers general questions and minor bugs.
