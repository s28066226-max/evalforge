# Northwind Cloud Pricing and Plans

## Plans

Northwind Cloud offers three plans: Starter, Team, and Enterprise. Starter costs $12 per user per month and includes 50 GB of storage per workspace. Team costs $29 per user per month and includes 1 TB of storage per workspace. Enterprise pricing is negotiated annually and has a minimum commitment of 100 seats.

## Billing

Monthly plans are billed on the first day of each calendar month. Annual plans receive a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can also be downloaded from the Billing page in the admin console.

Northwind accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Enterprise customers may also pay by bank transfer with net-30 terms. PayPal is not accepted.

## Free trial

Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is required to start a trial. When the trial ends, the workspace is downgraded to read-only mode until a plan is selected.

## Seats

Admins can add seats at any time; new seats are prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Removing seats takes effect at the next renewal date, not immediately.
